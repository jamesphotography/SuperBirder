相册识鸟 Photo ID

离线 CoreML 原生大模型，覆盖 10,964 种鸟类。智能主体感知裁切，结合 GPS 地理优先加权，深山无网也能 1 秒秒识。 Offline native CoreML model covering 10,964 bird species. Saliency auto-crop & GPS geo-weighting for instant offline identification.

⚡

闪识 · 手机实时 Live Camera ID

手机长焦镜头直接对准鸟类，FastViT-MA36 毫秒级动态追踪预测，自动语音播报鸟名，抬头观鸟无需频繁低头看屏。 Aim your iPhone camera at birds with FastViT-MA36 realtime tracking and automatic voice announcement—keep your eyes on the flock.

🔭

望远镜模式 Telescope Mode

USB-C / HDMI 采集卡直连大炮超长焦相机或单筒望远镜，1080p 60fps 实时高清取景，支持 1x-3x 视网膜数码变焦与一键截帧抓拍。 Connect spotting scopes or telephoto cameras via USB-C / HDMI. 1080p 60fps live view, 1x-3x retina zoom, and instant screenshot capture.

📷

联机拍 · 即拍即传 Camera Tethering

尼康 / 索尼等微单无线或有线直连，快门一按秒传手机自动识别；内置热点心跳保活防断连，支持二次自由手动框选精细裁切。 Direct Nikon/Sony tethering with auto instant ID. Built-in hotspot keep-alive and secondary manual cropping for camouflaged birds.

📝

边走边记 · 智能清单 Field Checklist

智能足迹与时间自动切分，按观测点聚类生成鸟种清单，一键打勾统计初见，彻底告别手写混乱记录。 Smart track and timeline auto-clustering by hotspots. One-tap checklist recording for seamless field birding without pen and paper.

皇冠徽章专属标记每一次生涯新鸟种首见（Lifer），见证并珍藏你与大自然的每一次珍贵初见。 Crown badge honors every life bird first sighting. Cherish every memorable encounter along your birding journey.

地图缩略图标记与探索导航，社区鸟讯互动点赞，支持 iCloud 自动多端云同步，多设备无缝切换。 Map thumbnail markers with waypoint navigation, community bird sightings, and seamless multi-device iCloud sync.
